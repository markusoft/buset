BUSET Privacy Policy

BUSET Free features & Full unlock — ease-z

Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to BUSET, including its Free features and optional Full unlock, published by ease-z. For privacy questions or support, email mark.prosoftphils@gmail.com. Effective date: 10 November 2026.

Offline play and local storage

Single-player games and the map builder run on your device. The app stores settings, player names, custom maps and drafts, saved game progress, and supported game replays in app storage. Custom-board identifiers and play counts are stored locally on your device to manage Free edition limits. These identifiers and counts are not sent to ease-z or other players. BUSET does not require an account and does not send this information to an ease-z server. We do not operate public online game servers in these editions.

Local multiplayer in BUSET Full

When you choose to host or join a LAN game, the participating devices exchange your chosen player name, room details, game actions and state, and connection or reconnection identifiers. Network connections expose local IP addresses to the host and participating devices. The host stores the room and game state, which can include player names and seat identifiers; names and actions can also appear in saved replays. These transfers are necessary to run the game and are directed by the players. ease-z does not receive or centrally store these LAN sessions. BUSET Free does not offer LAN play.

Camera and selected files

If you choose QR scanning, the camera is used to read a game invitation. Camera images are processed on your device for QR decoding and are not saved or uploaded by this feature. You can deny or revoke camera permission. When you select a board image for import, it is analyzed on your device. Import and export features use files you explicitly select or save. Map images and game files are not uploaded by BUSET; sharing an exported file through another app is your choice and is governed by that app too.

Security

Local games currently use HTTP and WebSocket connections without transport encryption. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network or a private hotspot and avoid untrusted public networks. Room passwords and seat reconnection identifiers are access controls, not encryption. Do not use sensitive personal information as a player name or room name. Camera permission is requested when needed.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

BUSET contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal data. The app does not use advertising identifiers or request location or contacts access. BUSET offers an optional one-time Full unlock through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and purchase-account information under its own policies. BUSET receives purchase status, a purchase token and a signed purchase record to verify, acknowledge and restore the unlock. The signed purchase record is stored locally for offline access and is not sent to ease-z. Purchase checks and acknowledgement communicate with Google Play when available. BUSET does not receive payment-card details. Refunded or revoked purchases may lose Full access when ownership is refreshed. Restoring a purchase does not restore maps or game saves deleted from your device.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is replaced, deleted using an available in-app control, cleared through Android app-storage settings, or the app is uninstalled. Clearing app storage removes saved games, settings, maps, custom-board play counts, cached purchase records and replays. Clearing local data does not cancel a Google Play purchase; restore the unlock with the purchasing Google Play account. Deleting a saved map alone does not clear its play count. Exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete them separately. A LAN host or another player may retain their own saved games or replays; ask them to delete their copies. ease-z cannot delete files stored solely on your device or another player's device. If you email support, we use the correspondence to answer your request and retain it only as needed for support, follow-up and applicable obligations; you may request deletion at the contact address.

Third-party services and this page

Google Play and Android may process information under their own policies. If you visit the published version of this policy on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process website access information under its privacy policy; the app itself does not load this page or trackers automatically. Opening email or choosing another app to share a file is optional.

Children and updates

The app does not create accounts or knowingly request children's personal details. Player names may be nicknames; avoid entering personal information. A parent or guardian can contact us about a privacy concern. If app behavior changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before or with the relevant release. The current policy is available from the app home page and Settings.
