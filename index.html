02 / BRING YOUR TABLE ALONG

Same room.
Same island.
No public server.

A router at home. A phone hotspot on a trip. BUSET Full lets an Android device host the game.

HOST⬡⬡FRIENDS

Connect to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, scan the host's QR code and take a seat. Fill the spare seats with bots, or let a bot take over when someone needs to leave.

LAN is a Full edition feature. Use a trusted local network that allows devices to communicate. LAN traffic is not encrypted. Public online play, chat and voice/video calls are not included.
